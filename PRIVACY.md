# PopTranslate — Privacy Policy

PopTranslate does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

When you select text on a webpage, that text is sent directly to the Google Translate API (translate-pa.googleapis.com) solely to retrieve a translation. If that request fails, the text is sent to the MyMemory translation API (mymemory.translated.net) instead. When you click the word details button for a single English word, that word is also sent to the Free Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev) to get its definition. If you click **Ask AI** and choose an assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok or Google AI Mode), a new tab opens on that website with the selected text in the question; that website's own privacy policy then applies. These are direct browser-to-API requests. PopTranslate itself does not see, store, log, or transmit your selected text to any server controlled by the developer.

Your preferences (source and target language, whether the popup is turned on, and the names of any sites where you turned it off) are saved locally using chrome.storage.sync, which syncs across your own Chrome profile only. This data never leaves your Google account.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party data sharing.
